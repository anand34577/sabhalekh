# Searching

The search box at the top of the sidebar looks through **every meeting** at once: transcripts and meeting titles.

![Search results](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anand34577/sabhalekh/main/docs/images/search-dark.png)

1. Click **Search all meetings…** and type.
2. Results show the meeting, the speaker and the matching words highlighted.
3. Click a result to open that meeting at that exact moment.

## English, Hindi and Hinglish

You can search the way you would type:

| You type | It finds |
|---|---|
| `budget` | budget, Budget, budgeting |
| `बजट` | बजट |
| `kya` | क्या |
| `sankat` | संकट |

Search ignores upper and lower case and the different ways Hindi text can be encoded, so a word copied from WhatsApp or
a website still matches.

**About romanised Hindi:** Sabhalekh converts Hindi to Roman letters with fixed rules, and people spell Hindi in many
ways. Common words match well, but a spelling that differs from the rules may not. For example `iran` does not find
`ईरान`, while `iraan` does. If a romanised search finds nothing, try another spelling or type the word in Devanagari.

## Filtering the list

Under the search box:

- **Filter** shows only finished, processing or failed meetings; English or Hindi meetings; recordings or imports;
  or meetings with a certain tag.
- **Date** changes the sort order.

## Searching inside one meeting

With a meeting open, press **Ctrl+F**. See [Working with transcripts](Working-with-Transcripts#finding-text-in-a-meeting).
